# Standard Korean Language Dictionary noun data

The `stdict-nouns-20260605.json.gz` word list and its source metadata are derived
from the **Standard Korean Language Dictionary (표준국어대사전)** by the
**National Institute of Korean Language (국립국어원)**.

Source: <https://stdict.korean.go.kr/>.
Public XML mirror: <https://github.com/spellcheck-ko/korean-dict-nikl>;
revision `42c0d01889f34536e9cf94fe57f62bd2055b1bde`, dictionary snapshot
**2026-06-05**. The mirror is independently maintained and is not operated by the
National Institute of Korean Language.

Changes: selected every entry with a noun (`명사`) part of speech, normalized
Unicode to NFC, removed dictionary component/optional-spacing markers (`-`, `^`)
and this snapshot's trailing two-digit homonym numbers, then deduplicated the
resulting surfaces in source order. Actual spaces, other characters and all noun
categories were retained. The 270,033 noun entries produce 213,810 unique words.
The accompanying metadata records the source files, hashes and extraction counts.
No definitions, examples or multimedia are included in this asset.
